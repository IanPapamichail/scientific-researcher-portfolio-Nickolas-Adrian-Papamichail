## Overview

This repository contains my personal scientific researcher portfolio, designed to present my academic background, research interests, technical projects, and interdisciplinary work across quantum computing, artificial intelligence, hybrid machine learning, and research-driven digital design.

The portfolio is built as a professional online presence for showcasing selected projects, publications, experimental ideas, visual research concepts, and future work in intelligent scientific systems.

## Research Areas

### Quantum Computing

My research interests include quantum algorithms, variational quantum circuits, quantum optimization, and hybrid quantum-classical systems.

### Hybrid Machine Learning

I explore architectures that combine classical machine learning with quantum-inspired or quantum-assisted computational models.

### Artificial Intelligence Systems

I work on intelligent systems for diagnostics, environmental intelligence, plant health analysis, and decision-support platforms.

### Research-Driven Design

This portfolio also reflects my interest in building clean, professional, and visually expressive scientific interfaces.
